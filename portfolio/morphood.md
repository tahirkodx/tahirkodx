# Morphood

**Any recipe. Morphood makes it fit the diet in the room.**

Live: [morphood.com](https://www.morphood.com) · [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/morphood-personalize-recipes/id1616706707) · [Open in portfolio](https://tahirkodx.github.io/tahirkodx/#morphood)

## Problem

A household rarely shares one diet. A recipe that works for one person can be unusable for the next. The product needed to adapt the dish, not just store it.

## What we discussed

Keep the loop short: take a recipe, adapt it, cook from the result. Nutrition and cook-mode sit next to the rewrite so the output is usable in the kitchen, not only on a screen.

## What shipped

- Web product at morphood.com
- iOS app on the App Store (Morphood: Personalize Recipes)
- Recipe transform for allergy, diet, or preference
- Nutrition breakdown and cook-mode

## How it was built

Vue.js and Nuxt.js on the web, with TanStack Query for server state. AI for the recipe transform. iOS app on the App Store.

## How it was deployed

Live homepage at morphood.com. iOS build on the App Store.
