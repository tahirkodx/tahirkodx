# Kuntokompassi Online

**A Finnish studio's coaching app. Live on the stores.**

Live: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/kuntokompassi-online/id6689495083) · [Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=fi.kuntokompassi.online.app) · [Open in portfolio](https://tahirkodx.github.io/tahirkodx/#kuntokompassi)

## Problem

The studio needed a member app that matches how they already coach, not a generic fitness template. It had to ship on both stores.

## What we discussed

Reuse the Quasar / Vue coaching patterns already proven on Trainero-family work. Wrap with Capacitor for App Store and Play. Keep plans, progress, and coach chat as the product surface.

## What shipped

- Kuntokompassi Online on the App Store and Google Play
- Plans, progress, coach chat
- Built for SHW Training Oy

## How it was built

Quasar / Vue 3 client, Pinia, Vue Router, ApexCharts, Vimeo player. Capacitor wrap for iOS and Android.

## How it was deployed

App Store (Kuntokompassi Online) and Google Play (`fi.kuntokompassi.online.app`). Studio marketing lives on Kajabi.
